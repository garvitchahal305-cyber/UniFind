Campus Safety & Security Notice

Unclaimed items turned in to Central Library or Student Union desks are transferred to the Campus Security Office (Building 4, Room 102) at the end of every week. Photo ID is mandatory for claiming electronics and wallets.

Read Campus Safety Guidelines How Pickup & Drop-off Works

Staffed Lost & Found Desks

  • Central Library: Floor 1 Help Desk (Mon–Sun: 8 AM – 11 PM)
  • Student Union: Info Center (Mon–Fri: 9 AM – 6 PM)
  • Campus Security: Bldg 4, Rm 102 (Open 24/7 All Year)

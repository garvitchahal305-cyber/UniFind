How UniFind Works

A transparent, student-first platform designed to quickly reunite misplaced belongings with their rightful owners across campus.

The 3-Step Process

Reuniting Belongings in 3 Easy Steps

Whether you've lost your dorm keys or found a scientific calculator in a lab, here is how the system works.

01

1. Submit a Report

Quickly fill out our straightforward report form. Specify whether you lost or found the item, select the campus building (e.g. Central Library, Science Complex), and provide identifying details.

  • Add a photo or choose a category preset
  • Mention subtle details like stickers or scratches
  • Specify precise areas (e.g. Desk 14, 2nd floor)
02

2. Search & Connect

Your report is immediately published to the live campus feed. Students and faculty can search by keywords, filter by building, or check recent postings from today.

  • Real-time keyword & category matching
  • Filter by campus zones and halls
  • Direct student email contact button
03

3. Verify & Reclaim

When a potential owner reaches out, verify ownership by requesting proof (like unlocking the phone, stating custom stickers, or presenting student ID).

  • Meet at designated public Campus Safe Zones
  • Hand in sensitive items to Campus Security (Rm 102)
  • Mark as "Reunited" once handed back

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I find a student ID card?

Please turn in Student ID cards immediately to the Campus Security Office (Building 4, Room 102) or the Registrar's Office. Security can look up the student in the university directory and dispatch an official university email notification to their inbox.

How do I prove an item belongs to me?

For electronic devices, you can unlock the passcode or match serial numbers. For bags or wallets, describe contents not shown in the public photos. For keys, describe specific tags or try them in person.

What happens to unclaimed items at the end of the term?

Campus Security retains unclaimed high-value items for 90 days. Unclaimed clothing and books are donated to local charity partners and campus student book exchanges at the end of each semester.

Can I offer a reward for my lost item?

Yes! When submitting a "Lost Item" report, there is an optional Reward field where you can mention cash, coffee treats, or gift cards to motivate finders.
